# Pitch notes for the Sora Skin demo

Five things to say to a business owner:

- "This is your shop in two languages. Tap the МН / EN switch and every page, price format and form switches with it."
- "It teaches before it sells. The routine panel shows a morning and evening routine for Mongolian winters, and one button adds the whole routine to the bag."
- "Customers can order from a phone in under two minutes: bag, Ulaanbaatar district and khoroo, pay on delivery. The order reaches you in Telegram."
- "It loads light on mobile data. Pages are prebuilt, images are small AVIF or WebP files, and the map only loads when someone taps it."
- "Everything about your business sits in a few files: name, phone, prices, hours, photos. Swapping my sample for your real shop takes about an hour, not weeks."

Show in this order: home, routine toggle, add routine, bag drawer, checkout with a Ulaanbaatar address, then open Telegram.
